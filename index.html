03 / Data preparation & statistics

Check the data. Keep the meaning.

Clean and normalize

Whitespace was trimmed and dates and numbers were parsed. The file has no missing values, duplicate rows, invalid categories, or nonpositive sales and units.

Min–max normalization places Sales and Unit on a 0–1 scale. Original values are retained for all revenue reports.

(value − minimum) / (maximum − minimum)

GroupBy splits records into meaningful groups for aggregation. A separate dataset would require a validated merge.

Distributions and potential outliers

High values beyond the box-plot whiskers are flagged for investigation, not automatically removed. Seaborn supports clear statistical plots; Matplotlib supplies layout and labels.

04 / Recommended next steps

Start focused. Measure the result.

VIC · strongest revenue

Validate expansion capacity.

Investigate store productivity, margin, and stock availability in Victoria before committing investment. A high revenue total alone cannot establish the best return.

WA · NT · TAS

Pilot local sales programs.

Test targeted bundles, loyalty offers, and inventory improvements in lower-revenue markets. Use a comparable untreated group and measure incremental revenue and margin after discounts.

Decision limits. The file has no store counts, costs, customer IDs, campaign exposure, or prior-year comparison. A single quarter cannot establish annual seasonality, causal campaign effects, or a reliable next-year forecast.
